## Modifications

The video files were shortened to two minutes, downscaled to at most 640 pixels
wide, and transcoded to H.264/AAC for efficient local testing. No additional
restrictions are applied. CC BY attribution must be retained when redistributing
the Big Buck Bunny or Sintel excerpts.

The Public Domain Mark is a rights statement rather than a license. Its use here
reflects the source archive's designation for those items.
